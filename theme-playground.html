How to apply this to your app
  1. Tap Copy CSS block above.
  2. In your GitHub repo, open index.html and tap the pencil to edit.
  3. Find the block near the top that starts with :root{.
  4. Replace the colour lines (from --iron down to --white-plate) with what you copied.
  5. Commit, wait a minute, refresh the app.
